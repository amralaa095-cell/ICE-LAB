!Safety first
  • Never hold the part by hand: always clamp it.
  • No gloves, loose sleeves, jewellery or open long hair.
  • Keep fingers away from the cutter, chips are sharp and hot.
  • Stop the machine before measuring or adjusting.
  • First time? Book an induction with a technician.

M-03 · Steps 1–6
Set up the mill
Circle = zoomed side view
Red border = stop and check
1
Gear up
Glasses on, hair tied, sleeves up, closed shoes. Take off gloves, rings and watch.
2
Clamp the part
Clamp the part, or a vice, to the T-slot table. Never hold it by hand.
3
Fit the cutter
Cutter into the collet, short stick-out, tighten the collet nut.
4
Line up
Z handwheel sets the cutter height, X moves the part beside the cutter.
5
Turn by handCHECK
Turn the spindle one full turn by hand: the cutter must clear everything.
6
Power on
Plug in and switch on. Hands off the table near the cutter.

M-03 · Steps 7–9
Cut, stop, finish
Circle = zoomed side view
Red border = stop and check
7
Touch off, set depth
Y handwheel in until the cutter just touches, zero the dial, add 0.1–0.2 mm.
8
Feed slowly
Wind X slowly against the cutter rotation. Several light passes.
9
Stop, measure, cleanCHECK
Switch off, wait for a stop, back away. Measure, then brush chips away.

Before you leave
Machine off and unplugged
Cutter removed and returned
