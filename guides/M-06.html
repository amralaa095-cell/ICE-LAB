Know your machine
1Motor · power drive
6Cross-slide handwheel · depth of cut
2Belt and pulleys · do not bend

33-jaw chuck
8Tailstock with live centre
4Workpiece
9In-line ON / OFF switch
5Tool post & cutting tool
!Safety first
  • Never leave the chuck key in the chuck.
  • No gloves, loose sleeves, jewellery or open long hair.
  • Never touch the spinning part, the belt or the chips.
  • Stop the machine before measuring or adjusting.
  • First time? Book an induction with a technician.

M-06 · Steps 1–6
Set up the lathe
Circle = zoomed side view
Red border = stop and check
1
Gear up
Glasses on, hair tied, sleeves up, closed shoes. Take off gloves, rings and watch.
2
Clamp the stock
Max 3× its diameter sticking out; longer parts need the tailstock centre.
3
Tool at centre height
The tool tip must sit exactly on the centre line of the part.
4
Key out, turn by handCHECK
Remove the chuck key. Turn the chuck one full turn: nothing should hit.
5
Power on
Plug in and switch on with the in-line switch. Stand to the side of the chuck.
6
Face the end
Touch the end, then wind the cross-slide across. 0.1–0.2 mm per pass.

M-06 · Steps 7–9
Turn, measure, finish
Circle = zoomed side view
Red border = stop and check
7
Turn the diameter
Set the depth with the cross-slide, wind the carriage toward the chuck. Repeat.
8
Stop, then measureCHECK
Switch off and wait for a full stop. Measure with the calipers.
9
Off, unplug, clean
Unplug, brush chips off the bed. Never use hands or compressed air.
If something goes wrong
ChatterLess stick-out, sharper tool, lighter cut
Pip left on the endTool not at centre height: reset it
Belt slips or squealsLighter cut; call a technician
Part slipsStop. Tighten all three jaws
Before you leave
Machine off and unplugged
Tool and chuck key returned
Chips brushed into the bin
Carriage wound back
Booking marked as finished
