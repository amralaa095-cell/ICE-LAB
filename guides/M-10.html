!Safety first
  • Never run the laser with the lid open.
  • Laser eyewear for 450 nm at all times.
  • Never leave the machine while it is cutting.
  • Never cut PVC, vinyl or unknown plastics: toxic fumes.
  • Fire or heavy smoke: stop, keep the lid closed, call a technician.

M-10 · Steps 1–6
Set up the laser
Circle = zoomed side view
Red border = stop and check
1
Gear up
Laser eyewear for 450 nm on. Hair tied, sleeves up, closed shoes.
2
Place the sheet
Lay it flat on the honeycomb. Approved materials only, never PVC.
3
Set the focus
Use the focus gauge between the nozzle and the sheet.
4
Close the lidCHECK
The lid stays closed whenever the laser can fire.
5
Air and extraction on
Switch on the air pump and the fume extraction before cutting.
6
Load the job
Open your file. Take speed and power from the lab material card.

M-10 · Steps 7–9
Cut, wait, finish
Circle = zoomed side view
Red border = stop and check
7
Frame the job
Run the outline first to check the job fits on the sheet.
8
Run, stay and watchCHECK
Never leave a running laser. Fire or smoke: stop and call a technician.
9
Wait, open, clean
Let the fumes clear, open the lid, remove the part and the scraps.
If something goes wrong
Does not cut throughCheck focus, lower the speed, add a pass
Burnt, dark edgesCheck the air pump, raise the speed
Job is off the sheetStop. Frame the job again before cutting
Flame or heavy smokeStop, keep the lid closed, call a technician
Before you leave
Laser and air pump off
Scraps removed from the honeycomb
Lid closed
Eyewear returned
Booking marked as finished
Book again & try the simulator
