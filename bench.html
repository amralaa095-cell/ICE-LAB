ICE Lab Virtual Bench

Simulations of the lab's own equipment, grouped by the section colours on the equipment tags. Each bench has the apparatus, live instrument readings, a logbook and the analysis students do in the lab.

Simulated readings include instrument noise. Specifications follow the manufacturers' datasheets where published; other values are typical textbook figures for teaching.
